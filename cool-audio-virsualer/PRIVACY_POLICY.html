Privacy Policy for cool audio visualizer

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how cool audio visualizer ("we", "our", or "the application") accesses, processes, and protects your data. We strictly respect your personal privacy and practice complete transparency.

Core Privacy Guarantee: cool audio visualizer never records, uploads, stores, or transmits your audio, microphone recordings, voice data, or personal files. All audio frequency calculations are ephemeral and discarded instantly.

1. Information We Access and Process

A. Audio Data (RECORD_AUDIO & AudioPlaybackCapture)

cool audio visualizer accesses audio input strictly to compute real-time Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) frequencies for neon equalizer bars, waves, and circular visualizers.

  • Ephemeral Processing: All frequency calculations take place purely in volatile system memory (RAM).
  • Zero Cloud / Zero Recording: No speech, music, or environmental sounds are ever saved to disk or transmitted over the network. Audio buffers are discarded instantly after each frame is drawn.

B. Device Preferences & Images

  • User settings, color palettes, and sensitivity adjustments are saved locally using Android's private SharedPreferences / DataStore.
  • Custom wallpapers and circle avatars chosen from your gallery remain strictly on your local device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

cool audio visualizer integrates Google AdMob to serve standard advertisements and rewarded ads (to unlock optional visual effects). Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (AAID), coarse IP location, and ad performance analytics under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Android Permissions Breakdown

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Required for the Android Visualizer API to process sound frequency.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Keeps the audio capture and floating visualizer running without interruption.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Allows rendering the visualizer overlay on top of other applications.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Used solely to detect when user-whitelisted music players are launched or closed for the App Filter automation feature. No activity history is saved or uploaded.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays foreground notifications on Android 13+.
  • INTERNET: Exclusively used by Google AdMob to load advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy

cool audio visualizer is designed for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, feel free to reach out via your developer support email or our project repository.
