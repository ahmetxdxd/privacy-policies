Privacy Policy for EasyStickers

Last updated: October 01, 2026

EasyStickers ("we", "our", or "us") provides a WhatsApp sticker maker application that allows users to create stickers from social media, web links, and local gallery media. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

EasyStickers is built with privacy in mind. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifying information like your name, email, phone number, or contacts.

  • Media & Images: Images, GIFs, and videos you select or import are processed entirely locally on your device (on-device AI background removal and sticker conversion). We do not upload or store your photos or sticker packs on any external server.
  • Advertising Identifiers: We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google Play Services may automatically collect advertising IDs, device model, operating system, and IP address for ad delivery and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's policies.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising) - Google Ads Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (GDPR / Consent Management)

3. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for downloading media from links and loading AdMob ads.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES & READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: Used solely to allow you to select photos or videos from your gallery to create stickers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Security

All sticker conversions, image processing, and sticker databases are kept strictly in your device's private app storage. When exporting to WhatsApp, stickers are shared locally via Android's secure ContentProvider.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
